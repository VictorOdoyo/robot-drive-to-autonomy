# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Initial public research release.

- Coupled battery, winding, wheel-slip, chassis and thermal simulation with energy accounting.
- Nested motor control and electrical wheel-speed fallback for encoder outages.
- Planar pose/bias estimation, occupancy mapping, route planning and supervised navigation.
- Eleven reproducible nominal and disturbed mission experiments.
- Sampled current-loop stability, coupled operating-point/LQR and offline mapping studies.
- Portable CLI, CSV/NPZ/HTML reporting, provenance and published scientific evidence.
